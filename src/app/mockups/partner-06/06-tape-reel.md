# Landing Design 06 - **Open to Collaborate**

A lighter social-network direction centered on visibility, availability, and music-community momentum. The page should feel active and optimistic, with a clean base and a strong accent color doing the emotional work.

## Direction

- Build around the idea that musicians here are present, open, and easy to discover.
- Make the experience feel social and current, not corporate or analytical.
- Keep the page easy to understand in seconds, with a sense of live network activity.

## Creative Freedom

- The layout, features, and network metaphors are intentionally flexible.
- Framer Motion, 3D, and other modern styling tools are encouraged if they make the community feel more alive.
- Avoid heavy professional-network stiffness or overbuilt product logic.
