# Landing Design 02 - **Studio Console**

A landing page that feels closer to professional music software than a typical startup site. The mood should be dark, precise, technical, and confident.

## Direction

- Borrow from studio gear, audio tools, and console interfaces.
- Let the page feel immersive and signal-driven rather than social or playful.
- The core experience should suggest serious music work and engineered craft.

## Creative Freedom

- Layout, features, and interactions are intentionally open-ended.
- Use modern styling, motion, or 3D libraries if they help sell the feeling.
- Keep the result restrained and avoid generic SaaS polish.
