# Landing Design 05 - **Profile Stack**

A light, social-first musician network where people and personality lead the page. It should feel polished and approachable, with a bold accent color and enough motion or depth to feel current.

## Direction

- Make profiles feel central, social, and music-native rather than corporate.
- Keep the page bright, friendly, and visually collectible, like a network you want to explore.
- The tone should balance professionalism with warmth and creative energy.

## Creative Freedom

- The exact composition, interaction model, and supporting elements are up to the agent.
- Use Framer Motion, 3D, or other modern styling tools if they help the profile surfaces feel alive.
- Keep it distinct from a generic professional network clone and avoid overly sterile UI.
