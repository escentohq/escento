# Design Doc: Soft Campus

## Goal

Create a friendly, approachable landing page for GigForge. This version should make reaching out to other students feel less intimidating while still avoiding social-network tropes.

## Visual Direction

- Style: soft campus collaboration tool.
- Mood: warm, safe, helpful, low-pressure.
- Background: pale blue-gray.
- Primary text: slate.
- Accent: soft green.
- UI treatment: simple rounded cards with light borders and subtle shadows.
- Typography: friendly sans-serif, bold headline.

## Page Structure

1. Layout:
   - Two-column desktop layout.
   - Left: positioning and CTAs.
   - Right: conversation-style cards.
   - Mobile: stack cleanly.

2. Pill label:
   - Rounded green pill.
   - Text:
     - `+ Built for students helping students`

3. Headline:
   - Large:
     - `Make campus collaboration feel less awkward.`

4. Supporting copy:
   - Explain that the design makes first contact approachable without making GigForge a social network.

5. CTAs:
   - Primary rounded dark button:
     - `Get started`
   - Secondary rounded outlined button:
     - `Browse first`

6. Conversation cards:
   - Three stacked cards with light border, white background, and very small radius.
   - Card 1:
     - Label: `+ Creator`
     - Text: `I need a warm piano theme for a documentary cut.`
   - Card 2:
     - Label: `+ Musician`
     - Text: `I play keys, score shorts, and can meet Thursdays.`
   - Card 3:
     - Label: `+ GigForge`
     - Text: `Portfolio link found. Email contact ready.`

## Implementation Notes

- Keep the cards supportive, not chat-app-like.
- Avoid avatars, DMs, feeds, likes, or social metrics.
- This should feel emotionally easy, not childish.
