# Design Doc: Bold Editorial

## Goal

Create a bold editorial landing page for GigForge, a student creator-to-student musician marketplace. The page should feel opinionated, print-like, and high contrast, while keeping the product promise simple: browse student musicians, post creative gigs, and contact directly by email.

## Visual Direction

- Style: brutalist editorial magazine page.
- Mood: confident, direct, slightly rebellious, but still product-clear.
- Background: neutral off-white or very light gray.
- Primary text: near-black.
- Accent: muted gray for italic secondary headline text.
- Typography: use a large serif display style for the hero and section headings. Body copy can remain readable and relaxed.
- Layout: strong horizontal rules, large whitespace, oversized headline, simple three-column pitch section.

## Page Structure

1. Hero section:
   - Full-width header with a heavy bottom border.
   - Small uppercase kicker: `Issue No. 01 - Spring`.
   - Huge headline:
     - `Book the band.`
     - second line italic and muted: `Skip the bullshit.`
   - Supporting copy explaining GigForge connects student musicians with student creators directly.
   - Two CTAs:
     - primary: `Sign in`
     - secondary: `Browse musicians ->`

2. Marquee section:
   - Black background with white oversized italic genre labels.
   - Horizontally scrolling or visually repeating list:
     - Jazz
     - Indie
     - Hip-Hop
     - Folk
     - Electronic
     - Classical
     - Punk
     - Soul
   - Use a hard bottom border or strong separation from adjacent sections.

3. Pitch section:
   - Three columns on desktop, stacked on mobile.
   - Each column has a top border, mono number label, bold title, and body copy.
   - Columns:
     - `01 / For creators`
     - `02 / For musicians`
     - `03 / For the scene`

## Implementation Notes

- Avoid rounded, glossy SaaS cards.
- Use borders and typography as the main visual devices.
- Keep CTAs rectangular and compact.
- Keep the page direct and sparse; do not add decorative illustrations.
- Mobile should stack sections cleanly, with the hero headline still oversized but not overflowing.
