# Landing Design 07 - **Campus Network**

A brighter campus-social interpretation of GigForge: musicians, schools, and activity all feeling connected in one living system. It should feel credible, but also energetic and easy to imagine students actually using every day.

## Direction

- Blend school identity, network energy, and musician discovery in a lighter visual language.
- Let the page feel active, connected, and community-driven rather than institutional or analytical.
- Use a clean base with a memorable accent color and a sense of movement.

## Creative Freedom

- The specific visual metaphor, layout, and feature set are up to the agent.
- Modern motion, 3D, mapping, or spatial ideas are welcome if they help the network feel alive.
- Avoid making it feel like an admin tool, a heavy dashboard, or a generic social feed.
