# Landing Design 04 - **Stagelight**

A brighter, more music-social take on performance energy. It should feel like musicians are stepping into view, with light, motion, and a strong accent color creating a sense of live presence.

## Direction

- Borrow from stagecraft and performance, but keep the page lighter, cleaner, and more inviting than a dark venue poster.
- Make the experience feel social, immediate, and built around people being seen.
- Let visual drama come from motion, lighting, and composition rather than heaviness.

## Creative Freedom

- The signature visual, layout, and feature set are intentionally open.
- Framer Motion, 3D, and other modern visual tooling are encouraged if they help create stage energy.
- Keep it elegant and musician-focused; avoid turning it into a gloomy concert flyer or a generic marketing splash.
