# Landing Design 03 - **Paper & Ink**

A lighter, more social version of GigForge that still feels editorial and music-aware. Think warm paper, living activity, and a strong accent color that gives the page energy without losing taste.

## Direction

- Keep it soft, bright, and human, but make it feel like an active network for musicians rather than a static publication.
- Let typography, movement, and a warm accent color carry the identity.
- The page should feel alive, friendly, and art-directed instead of analytical.

## Creative Freedom

- The exact layout, social surfaces, and interaction model are up to the agent.
- Use Framer Motion, 3D, or other modern styling tools if they make the page feel more alive.
- Keep the experience light, contemporary, and musician-first rather than blog-like or product-demo heavy.
